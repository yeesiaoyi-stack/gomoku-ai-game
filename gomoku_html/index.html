GOMOKU

How to Play

Goal: Connect 5 stones in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal).

⚫ Black stone always plays first. ⚪ White stone plays second.

Strategy:

  • Build open 3 or open 4 lines to create winning chances
  • Block opponent’s threats before they become dangerous
  • Create double threats (forks) to force opponent into defense
  • Balance between attack and defense — don’t only focus on blocking

Tip: Center control is important. Controlling the middle gives more winning directions.
